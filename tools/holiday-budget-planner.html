    Start with one number

    The most effective holiday budget starts with a single total that you can pay from savings and this month's income, without a card balance that lingers into the new year. Everything else is a way of dividing that number. Set the total first, then fill in the gifts and the other categories, and adjust until Left to allocate is zero or positive.

    The costs people forget

    Gifts are the obvious part, but the extras are often what pushes a holiday over budget: food and drinks for several days, the tree and decorations, wrapping paper, cards and postage, travel to family, parties and office collections, and charity donations. Listing them separately makes them visible early, while there is still time to cut back.

    Setting gift amounts

    • Decide the gift total before choosing individual gifts, then divide it per person.
    • Agree on a limit or a Secret Santa with large families or groups of friends.
    • Track what you actually spend against each amount. A planner that shows budget against actual makes overspending obvious.

    Saving week by week

    The planner counts the days until the date you choose (Christmas Day by default) and divides what you still need by the remaining weeks. Setting that amount aside each week, for example by moving it to a separate savings pot on payday, spreads the cost and avoids a January surprise. If the weekly amount looks too high, lower the total now rather than borrowing later. Our Christmas budget guide walks through a full example.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much should I spend on Christmas?

    There is no universal right amount. A practical rule is to spend only what you can cover from savings and current income without carrying a credit card balance into the new year.

    How do I avoid overspending on gifts?

    Set a total gift budget first, give each person a limit, keep a list and record what you actually spend. Group agreements such as a Secret Santa also help.

    Can I use this for other holidays?

    Yes. Change the holiday date and rename the gift list; the categories work for birthdays, Eid, Hanukkah, Diwali or a family trip.

    How is the weekly saving calculated?

    Planned spending minus what you have already set aside, divided by the number of weeks left until the holiday date (rounded up).

    Is my gift list private?

    Yes. It is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to Market Plus.
